# ATORVASTATIN CALCIUM - Statin (HMG-CoA Reductase Inhibitor)
Source: DailyMed (NIH/NLM), setid=f7e8c8d2-b74e-6afd-e053-6394a90af83e
URL: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=f7e8c8d2-b74e-6afd-e053-6394a90af83e

## INDICATIONS AND USAGE
HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor (statin) indicated to reduce risk of MI, stroke, revascularization, and angina in adults with CHD risk factors or established CHD; as adjunct to diet for primary hyperlipidemia, HeFH (age 10+), HoFH (age 10+, with other LDL-lowering therapies), dysbetalipoproteinemia, and hypertriglyceridemia.

## CONTRAINDICATIONS
- Acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis.
- Hypersensitivity to atorvastatin or any excipient.

## WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS
- **Myopathy/Rhabdomyolysis**: risk factors are age >=65, uncontrolled hypothyroidism, renal impairment, concomitant interacting drugs, higher dose. Discontinue if markedly elevated CK or myopathy suspected.
- **Immune-Mediated Necrotizing Myopathy (IMNM)**: rare autoimmune myopathy, can persist after discontinuation.
- **Hepatic dysfunction**: transaminase elevations; contraindicated in acute liver failure/decompensated cirrhosis.
- Increases in HbA1c/fasting glucose reported.
- Increased hemorrhagic stroke risk at 80mg dose in patients with recent hemorrhagic stroke (SPARCL trial).

## DRUG INTERACTIONS
**Not recommended concomitantly** (myopathy/rhabdo risk): cyclosporine, gemfibrozil, tipranavir+ritonavir, glecaprevir+pibrentasvir.
**Dose caps due to interaction:**
- Clarithromycin or itraconazole: do not exceed atorvastatin 20 mg/day.
- Saquinavir+ritonavir, darunavir+ritonavir, fosamprenavir (+/- ritonavir), elbasvir+grazoprevir, letermovir: do not exceed 20 mg/day.
- Nelfinavir: do not exceed 40 mg/day.
**Risk/benefit consideration needed:** lopinavir+ritonavir, simeprevir, ledipasvir+sofosbuvir, niacin (>=1g/day), other fibrates, colchicine.
**Grapefruit juice**: avoid >1.2 L/day — raises atorvastatin plasma levels, increases myopathy risk.
**Rifampin**: reduces atorvastatin levels via CYP3A4 induction; administer SIMULTANEOUSLY (not staggered) to avoid loss of efficacy.
**Atorvastatin's effect on other drugs**: increases oral contraceptive (norethindrone/ethinyl estradiol) levels; increases digoxin plasma levels — monitor digoxin patients. No clinically significant effect on warfarin prothrombin time.

## ADVERSE REACTIONS (>=5%, any dose vs placebo)
Nasopharyngitis (8.3%), arthralgia (6.9%), diarrhea (6.8%), pain in extremity (6.0%), UTI (5.7%).

## DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION
Adults: start 10-20 mg once daily (up to 40mg if needing >45% LDL reduction); range 10-80 mg once daily, with or without food. Reassess LDL-C at 4 weeks.
Pediatric HeFH (10-17y): start 10 mg/day, range 10-20mg.
Pediatric HoFH (10y+): start 10-20mg/day, range 10-80mg.
Renal impairment: no dosage adjustment needed (renal disease doesn't affect atorvastatin plasma levels), but renal impairment IS a myopathy risk factor requiring monitoring.
Hepatic impairment: contraindicated in acute liver failure/decompensated cirrhosis; markedly increased exposure in chronic alcoholic liver disease (Child-Pugh A: 4x Cmax/AUC; Child-Pugh B: ~16x Cmax, 11x AUC).

## USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS
Discontinue when pregnancy recognized — may cause fetal harm (cholesterol synthesis inhibition). Breastfeeding not recommended during treatment.
Geriatric (>=65): risk factor for myopathy — cautious dose selection, monitor.
