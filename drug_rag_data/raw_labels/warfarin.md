# WARFARIN SODIUM - Anticoagulant (Vitamin K Antagonist)
Source: DailyMed (NIH/NLM) - Warfarin Sodium Tablets, Initial U.S. Approval 1954
URL: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=b1b766b1-0327-48cd-ab01-e85038cf365f

## INDICATIONS AND USAGE
Vitamin K antagonist indicated for:
- Prophylaxis and treatment of venous thrombosis and its extension, pulmonary embolism (PE).
- Prophylaxis and treatment of thromboembolic complications associated with atrial fibrillation (AF) and/or cardiac valve replacement.
- Reduction in risk of death, recurrent MI, and thromboembolic events (stroke, systemic embolization) after myocardial infarction.
Note: has no direct effect on an established thrombus, does not reverse ischemic tissue damage.

## BOXED WARNING (implied by "can cause major or fatal bleeding")
Warfarin sodium can cause major or fatal bleeding. Bleeding more likely in the first month of therapy. Risk factors: high intensity anticoagulation (INR >4.0), age >=65, history of highly variable INRs, history of GI bleeding, hypertension, cerebrovascular disease, anemia, malignancy, trauma, renal impairment, certain genetic factors, certain concomitant drugs, long duration of therapy.
Perform regular INR monitoring in all treated patients.

## MONITORING
Narrow therapeutic index drug — action affected by other drugs and dietary vitamin K. Determine INR daily after initial dose until stable, then periodically (generally every 1-4 weeks). Additional INR checks needed whenever other medications are started/stopped/taken irregularly. Heparin increases INR.

## DRUG INTERACTIONS (Section 7 - central to this drug's hallucination-risk profile)
Mechanisms: pharmacodynamic (synergism via impaired hemostasis or reduced clotting factor synthesis; competitive antagonism of vitamin K; hereditary resistance) and pharmacokinetic (enzyme induction/inhibition, altered plasma protein binding).
CYP450 isozymes metabolizing warfarin: CYP2C9 (more potent S-enantiomer), CYP1A2 and CYP3A4 (R-enantiomer).
- **Inhibitors of CYP2C9/1A2/3A4** → increase warfarin exposure → increase INR/bleeding risk.
- **Inducers of CYP2C9/1A2/3A4** → decrease warfarin exposure → decrease INR/reduce anticoagulant effect (clot risk).
- **Drugs increasing bleeding risk directly** (independent of PK): antiplatelet agents, NSAIDs, other anticoagulants — closely monitor when combined.
- **Antibiotics/antifungals**: reports of INR changes exist even without consistent PK-level effects — closely monitor INR when starting/stopping any antibiotic or antifungal.
- Botanical/herbal products can also affect INR — consult labeling of all concurrent drugs.
- Note: exact drug-by-drug interaction lists in the full label's Table 2/Table 3 should always be consulted per-drug; this is a narrow-therapeutic-index drug where individualized dose titration and monitoring override any fixed rule.

## ADVERSE REACTIONS
Most common: fatal and nonfatal hemorrhage from any tissue or organ.

## USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS
Pregnancy: contraindicated except pregnant women with mechanical heart valves, where benefits may outweigh risks (warfarin can cause fetal harm).
Lactation: monitor breastfeeding infants for bruising/bleeding.
Renal impairment: instruct patients to monitor INR more frequently.
