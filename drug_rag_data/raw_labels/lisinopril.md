# LISINOPRIL - ACE Inhibitor (Antihypertensive)
Source: DailyMed (NIH/NLM), Lisinopril Tablets, Initial U.S. Approval 1988
URL: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=8f20acd7-2635-4a9b-b732-2a84ea93dea7

## INDICATIONS AND USAGE
ACE inhibitor indicated for treatment of hypertension in adults and pediatric patients 6 years and older; also used as adjunctive therapy for systolic heart failure with diuretics and (usually) digitalis.

## BOXED WARNING - FETAL TOXICITY
When pregnancy is detected, discontinue lisinopril as soon as possible. Drugs acting directly on the renin-angiotensin system can cause injury and death to the developing fetus (2nd/3rd trimester use reduces fetal renal function, increases fetal/neonatal morbidity/death).

## CONTRAINDICATIONS
- History of angioedema or hypersensitivity related to previous ACE inhibitor treatment.
- Hereditary or idiopathic angioedema.
- **Contraindicated in combination with a neprilysin inhibitor** (e.g., sacubitril) — do not administer within 36 hours of switching to/from sacubitril/valsartan.
- Do not co-administer aliskiren with lisinopril in patients with diabetes.

## WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS
- Angioedema (face, extremities, lips, tongue, glottis, larynx) can occur at any time during treatment, including fatal reactions. Promptly discontinue and monitor until resolution. Higher rate reported in Black vs non-Black patients.
- Patients on mTOR inhibitors (temsirolimus, sirolimus, everolimus) or neprilysin inhibitors: increased angioedema risk.
- Symptomatic hypotension possible, especially in volume/salt-depleted patients (e.g., those on diuretics or dialysis).
- Hyperkalemia risk — advise against potassium-containing salt substitutes without consulting physician.
- Leukopenia/neutropenia — report signs of infection (sore throat, fever).

## DRUG INTERACTIONS
- **Neprilysin inhibitors (sacubitril)**: contraindicated combination — angioedema risk.
- **Aliskiren**: contraindicated in diabetic patients.
- **Diuretics**: excessive hypotension risk, especially first dose, in volume-depleted patients.
- **Oral antidiabetic agents / insulin**: increased hypoglycemia risk in first month of combined ACE inhibitor use — monitor closely.
- **NSAIDs**: may reduce antihypertensive effect and worsen renal function (well-established ACE-inhibitor class interaction).
- **Potassium-sparing diuretics, potassium supplements, salt substitutes**: hyperkalemia risk.
- **Lithium**: ACE inhibitors can increase serum lithium levels (class effect, monitor lithium levels).

## DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION
Hypertension (adults): initial 10 mg once daily; titrate up to 40 mg/day based on response. Patients on diuretics: start 5 mg once daily to reduce hypotension risk.
Pediatric (6y+, GFR >30 mL/min/1.73m2): initial 0.07 mg/kg (max 5mg) once daily.
Heart failure: initiate 5 mg once daily; with hyponatremia (Na <130 mEq/L) start 2.5 mg once daily; titrate to max 40 mg once daily.

## ADVERSE REACTIONS
Syncope (0.8% in combination product vs 0.1% lisinopril alone in some formulations), cough (ACE-inhibitor class effect), hyperkalemia, hypotension, renal function changes, angioedema (rare but serious).

## USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS
Pregnancy: Contraindicated after first trimester — fetal toxicity (boxed warning).
Lactation: Advise against breastfeeding during treatment.
